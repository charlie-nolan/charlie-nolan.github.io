How I work

Rules I don't break

  • You can watch. Stand there, ask questions, take photos. I'd rather you see it than wonder.
  • Jack stands, chocks, torque wrench. Never under a car on a jack alone. Lug nuts torqued to spec and written on your invoice.
  • Old parts go back to you if you want them. You'll know the work was done.
  • I tell you what I find, then leave it alone. If your rear pads are getting thin, I'll show you and quote it. No pressure, no scare tactics.
  • If a job isn't right for a driveway, I say so. Timing belts, transmissions, A/C and airbags go to a shop. I'll tell you which one.
  • Clean driveway when I leave. Drain pan, absorbent, and your used oil goes to the recycler.

Buying a used car?

Pre-purchase inspection, at the seller's location

An hour on the car before you hand over cash: codes (including recently cleared ones), leaks, rust, accident repair, brake and tire life, test drive. You get a written checklist with photos and my honest take on what it's worth.

Questions

Before you text

Can I supply my own parts?

Yes. You pay labor only. I'll tell you exactly which part to buy. Parts you supply aren't covered by my warranty, but my labor is.

What if it's raining?

It's Shoreline. I bring a canopy. Heavy wind or a sloped or gravel driveway means we reschedule or find flat pavement nearby.

Where can you work?

Any flat, level, paved spot where you're allowed to park: driveway, apartment lot, workplace lot. Not on the street in traffic.

What cars do you work on?

Mostly 1990s to mid-2010s Honda, Toyota, Subaru, Ford, Chevy, Mazda, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, and light trucks. European and hybrid cars: text me and I'll say yes or no honestly.

How do I pay?

Card, cash, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, when the job is done. For parts over $100 I ask for a deposit before I buy them.

Is there a warranty?

My labor is guaranteed. If something I installed is wrong, I come back and fix it. Parts I supply carry the parts store's warranty.
