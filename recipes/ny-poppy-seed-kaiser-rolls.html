NY poppy seed kaiser rolls

A clone attempt at the New York deli kaiser roll, with a shatter crust from steam.

Serves ~10 rolls · Overnight cold retard

Ingredients (grams)

  • 595 g high-gluten flour (All Trumps)
  • 340 g water (cold tap or ice water)
  • 9 g instant dry yeast (roughly 1 tablespoon)
  • 7 g fine sea salt (puts you exactly at a 1.2% baker's percentage)
  • 14 g diastatic malt powder (do not exceed)
  • 14 g white sugar
  • 28 g lard (room temperature paste)
  • 28 g egg white (roughly 1 large egg white, zero yolk)
  • For dusting: 50/50 mix of potato starch and AP flour
  • For topping: plain water in a spray bottle, poppy seeds

The formula in ounces (yield: ~10 rolls)

  • 21 oz high-gluten flour (All Trumps)
  • 12 oz water (cold tap or ice water, do not use warm)
  • 1 tbs instant dry yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 oz diastatic malt powder (do not exceed)
  • 0.5 oz white sugar
  • 1 oz lard (room temperature paste, pass the smell test first)
  • 1 oz egg white (roughly 1 large egg white, zero yolk)
  • For dusting: white rice flour (or a 50/50 mix of AP flour and cornstarch)
  • For topping: plain water in a spray bottle, poppy seeds

Steps

  1. The autolyse (the rest). In a large bowl, whisk the cold water and yeast together. Add the flour, salt, diastatic malt, sugar, room-temperature lard, and egg white. Mix with a sturdy spoon or your hands just until there is no dry flour left. It will look shaggy and stiff. Cover the bowl with a damp towel and walk away for 30 minutes to let the 14.2% protein absorb the water.
  2. The slap-and-fold (mechanical kneading). Turn the dough out onto an un-floured surface. Pick it up, slap the bottom edge against the counter, stretch the top toward you, and fold it over itself.
  3. The room-temp ferment and coil folds. Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl. Let it ferment at room temperature for 2 hours. At the 1-hour mark, wet your hands, lift the dough from the middle to stretch it, and fold the ends under (a coil fold). Repeat this fold at the 90-minute mark, and again at the 2-hour mark to build structural tension.
  4. The cold retard (8 to 12 hours max). After the final fold, punch the dough down to degas it. Cover the bowl tightly with plastic wrap and place it in the refrigerator overnight. This slows the yeast while allowing the amylase enzymes to build the crust sugars.
  5. Scaling and bench rest (preventing rhino skin). Take the dough out of the fridge and let it sit for 30 minutes to take the chill off. Scale the dough into 3.25 oz pieces and shape them into tight balls. You must cover them immediately with a damp towel or plastic wrap for a 30-minute bench rest so the gluten relaxes without forming a dry skin.
  6. Shaping and proofing (the clopping method). Dust your workbench and the top of the dough generously with your rice flour (or AP/cornstarch mix). Flatten a ball into a 6-inch disc. Place your left thumb in the center. Fold a section of dough over your thumb and press it down. Continue folding sections over, overlapping them, moving clockwise 4 times. Take the final tail piece, tuck it deeply into the hole where your thumb is, and press firmly to anchor it. Proof them upside-down on a damp towel for about 30 to 45 minutes until almost doubled.
  7. The bake (the steam trap), 450°F. While they proof, let the oven preheat for a full 45 minutes to heat-saturate the baking steel. Gently flip the rolls right-side up onto parchment paper. Mist them lightly with the water spray bottle and sprinkle with poppy seeds. Load them onto the steel. Immediately pour 1 cup of boiling water into the lava rocks and shut the door quickly.
  8. The vent and finish. Bake with heavy steam for exactly 5 minutes. (This builds the shatter-crust.) After 5 minutes, carefully open the oven door for a few seconds to vent out the remaining steam. Reduce the oven temperature to 400°F and bake for another 15 minutes until deep golden brown. Let cool on a wire rack.

Notes from my last bake

  • 125 grams per roll.
  • Proofed after stamping and seeding, right side up, on parchment paper, with a cutting board and medium baking tray to weight it down.
  • 5 mins in steam at 425, 10 mins at 400 (oven probe reading 360–400).
