Queso de freír

My 2-gallon master protocol: gentle grain, precompression, a cheese that fries without melting.

Serves ~2.5 lb block · About 5 hours, plus overnight

2-Gallon Master Protocol (Gentle Grain & Precompression Method)

Target Yield: ~1,135 g (2.50 lbs) | Finished Block: 7.75" × 4.0" × 2.0" | Target pH: 6.3–6.5

1. Ingredient Weights & Volumes

Ingredient Weight (Grams) Volume Equivalent Prep Notes
Pasteurized Whole Milk 7,760 g 2 Gallons (7.57 L) Standard HTST pasteurized & homogenized. Never Ultra-Pasteurized (UHT).
Calcium Chloride (30%) 3.0 g 1/2 tsp (2.5 mL) Dilute in 50 g (1/4 cup) cool, unchlorinated water before adding.
NEC Mesophilic Culture 0.223 g Exactly 1/2 of one sachet Target weight locked for perfect flavor balance without over-acidifying.
Liquid Animal Rennet 5.2 g 1 tsp (5.0 mL) Dilute in 50 g (1/4 cup) cool, unchlorinated water right before use.
Diamond Crystal Kosher Salt 46.0 g ~4.5 tbsp Applied directly to warm cubed curds.

2. Time & Temperature Schedule

Elapsed Time Step Target Temp Duration Action & Mechanical Cues
0:00 – 0:20 1. Warm & Restore CaCl_2 90°F (32.2°C) 20 min Warm milk gently over medium-low heat (stirring often so bottom doesn't scorch). At 88°F–90°F, stir in diluted 3.0 g Calcium Chloride for 30 sec.
0:20 – 0:42 2. Inoculate & Short Ripen 90°F (32.2°C) 22 min Sprinkle 0.223 g Mesophilic culture across milk surface. Wait 2 min to rehydrate, stir up-and-down for 60 sec, then hold covered at 90°F for 20 min max.
0:42 – 0:52 3. Ramp to Rennet Temp 100°F (37.8°C) 10 min Gently warm milk from 90°F up to 100°F (~1°F per minute). Turn burner completely OFF.
0:52 – 1:32 4. Coagulation Rest 100°F (37.8°C) 40 min Pour in diluted 5.2 g Animal Rennet. Stir with vertical up-and-down strokes for 45 sec, hold spoon flat for 10 sec to stop all swirling, cover pot, and leave undisturbed for 40 min until a finger test gives a sharp, clean break.
1:32 – 1:47 5. Gentle Break & Heal 98°F–100°F (37°C) 15 min 0–4 min: With clean hands or a long knife, slowly and gently break the firm gel into large, walnut-to-golf-ball-sized chunks (1 to 1.5 inches). 4–15 min: Leave the pot completely untouched for 10+ min so the cut outer surfaces heal and the large curds firm up and sink.
1:47 – 2:05 6. High-Temp Scald Ramp 100°F ➔ 122°F (37.8°C ➔ 50.0°C) 18 min Turn heat to low. Raise temp to 122°F over 18 min (~1.2°F/min). Fold gently from the bottom up with your hand or a skimmer so the large curds warm evenly without shattering or matting to the floor.
2:05 – 2:15 7. Scald Hold (Kill & Shrink) 120°F–122°F (49°C–50°C) 10 min Hold between 120°F and 122°F for 10 min, folding gently. The large curds will shrink slightly, case-hardening their outer skins while keeping their internal moisture (halting the bacteria so pH stays >6.3).
2:15 – 2:20 8. Precompression Slab Formation 115°F–120°F 5 min (Optional: harvest 2 cups of pure whey now if making a brine soak-highly reccomended). Ladle off the whey until only a 1-inch pool remains. Lay a flat plate directly on top of the submerged curds in the pot. Apply steady upper-body pressure (15–20 lbs) straight down for 4–5 min until the curds fuse into a flat, uniform 2-inch slab.
2:20 – 2:27 9. Slice, Salt & Sweat 106°F–110°F (41°C–43°C) 7 min Drain the pot completely. Slide the warm curd slab onto a cutting board and slice into a 3/4-inch grid. While still warm, sprinkle the 46.0 g Diamond Kosher salt over the cubes in three stages, tossing gently by hand. The salt will rapidly draw out moisture, mixing with the butterfat to coat the curds in a glossy film of salted whey.
2:27 – 2:57 10. Initial Press ~105°F (40.5°C) going in 30 min Line the 2-gallon mold with damp butter muslin. Pack the warm, glossy salted curds firmly into corners, fold cloth flat across top, insert follower plate, and press under 20–25 lbs (9.0–11.5 kg) of weight for 30 minutes.
2:57 – 4:57 11. Flip & Final Press Room Temp (68°F–72°F) 120 min Remove weight and follower, pull block out by the cloth, unwrap, flip block 180° upside down, pull cloth taut around sides to erase creases, re-insert into mold, and press under the same 20–25 lbs (9.0–11.5 kg) for 2 hours.
Overnight 12. Dry Cold Set 36°F–40°F (2°C–4°C) 12+ hrs Unmold (~1,135 g finished brick), pat surface completely dry with paper towels, wrap loosely in a single dry paper towel inside a Ziploc bag (air pressed out), and refrigerate overnight. Note: For the v1 taste-test flavor, let it age dry for 2-3 days, then soak it in a reserved jar of salted whey for 8-12 hours before frying.
