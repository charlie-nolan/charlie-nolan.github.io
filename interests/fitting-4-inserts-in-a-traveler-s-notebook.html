How I fit 4 inserts plus a card wallet in my Traveler's Notebook with almost no overhang.

My Traveler's Notebook carries 4 inserts (the x4 and wallet, plus 3-4 watercolor paper inserts) and my cards in a clear vinyl wallet, and it closes with only a tiny bit of overhang.

How it works

  • The vinyl card wallet doesn't get its own elastic wrapped around it. The spine elastic runs through it instead.
  • I melted holes through the fold of the vinyl wallet with a soldering iron.
  • Melting instead of punching leaves a thickened bead of vinyl around each hole. That makes the hole stronger, and the rounded edge doesn't cut or fray the elastic.
  • With the wallet threaded on the elastic, it stops adding bulk at the fold, so everything closes nearly flush.
  • A binder clip on the cover keeps it shut.
